# Music Direction

## Tone

**One continuous ambient bed** that sits under the entire 1:45. Not a track that builds. Not a track with drops. A texture.

Think:
- A military procedural opening (Sicario, Tehran, Fauda's quieter moments)
- An operational briefing room
- Air conditioning hum but musical
- "We're inside the situation room" — not "we're about to win the game"

Do NOT think:
- Trailer trap
- "Epic" cinematic stings
- Tech startup ambient (you'll know the sound — soft synth pads with airy strings)
- Inspirational corporate

## Target levels

| Element | LUFS |
|---|---|
| Voiceover | -16 LUFS |
| Music bed | -22 LUFS |
| UI taps / SFX | -28 LUFS |
| Master output | -16 LUFS integrated, -1 dBFS true peak |

The music never competes with the VO. The VO is the voice of the operator; the music is the room they're in.

## Where to source

**Royalty-free / commercial license** options. All have catalogs that match the tone:

- **Artlist** — search: *ambient tactical*, *military procedural*, *briefing room*, *operational underscore*
- **Musicbed** — search: *neutral cinematic ambient*, *documentary procedural*
- **Soundstripe** — category: *Drama / Cinematic / Ambient*
- **Epidemic Sound** — search: *modern ambient*, *tactical*

Avoid:
- Pond5's "trailer" category
- Anything tagged "epic" / "motivational" / "uplifting"
- Anything with a drum buildup at 0:30

## Mix notes

- **No buildup**: keep the track at the same dynamic intensity throughout. If your chosen track builds, mute the build sections.
- **Loop if needed**: 1:45 is short enough that a 1:00 track looped + crossfaded works. Editor's call.
- **Sidechain duck**: -4 to -6 dB duck on VO trigger. Not the modern dance-music sidechain — a gentle "make room" duck.
- **Fade in / out**: 1.5s fade-in over Scene 1, 2.5s fade-out under the final "הפלוגה שלי" line.
- **Sub-frequency hygiene**: high-pass at 80 Hz on the music bed. The mobile speakers most viewers use can't reproduce sub-bass anyway.

## What "done" sounds like

Close your eyes during playback. You should hear:
1. A calm voice telling you a story
2. A room around the voice — present, not playing for attention
3. Occasional clicks when the screen changes
4. Silence at exactly the right moments — especially the 2s before "הפלוגה שלי"

If you hear the music more than you hear the voice, redo the mix.
